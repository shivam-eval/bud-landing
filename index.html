FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Bud?

Bud is your personal health companion. It starts with a health assessment, turns your assessment and test results into a personal nutrition and wellness plan, and connects you with doctors and dietitians. Then it supports you every day, tracking your food, supplements and physical activity with nudges and reminders, and checks in on your progress along the way.

Do I need to download an app?

No. You talk to Bud on WhatsApp, an app you already use, so there's nothing new to download.

Can I do it with my friends?

Yes. Ask Bud to "invite buddies" and it gives you a link to forward to friends or share in a WhatsApp group. Everyone who joins does Bud's 21-day challenge with you - log one meal a day on any 21 of 30 days - and you all see each other's progress, daily calories and protein on one leaderboard. Never what anyone ate, their weight or their phone number.

Does Bud work for people on GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Wegovy or Mounjaro?

Yes. Bud plans around a smaller appetite, sizes food suggestions to match, and can remind you when your next dose is due. It's not medical advice - talk to your doctor about your treatment.

How do I get started?

Tap "Start the wellness assessment" on this page, or message Bud on WhatsApp at wa.me/15672352765 and say hi. No signup needed.

Is my data private?

Yes - see our Privacy Policy for exactly what Bud collects, how it's used, and how to delete it at any time.

How does Bud work out my calorie and protein targets?

From your age, sex, height, weight, how active you are and your goal, using ICMR-NIN's 2020 method for Indians and WHO's limits for fat and fibre. The full method, every source and a calculator are on our blog.
