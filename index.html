Frequently asked questions

What is Bud?

Bud is a nutrition copilot you use entirely inside WhatsApp - no app to download. Send a photo of a meal or just say what you ate, and Bud logs the calories and protein, finds food nearby that fits your diet, and supports GLP-1 programs.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Bud works entirely over WhatsApp - just message the number and that's it.

Is Bud free?

Yes, Bud is free during this private preview. If that ever changes, you'll get clear notice before anything is charged.

Does Bud work for people on GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Wegovy or Mounjaro?

Yes. Bud sizes food suggestions for a smaller appetite and can remind you when your next dose is due. It's not medical advice - talk to your doctor about your treatment.

How do I get access?

Just message Bud on WhatsApp - tap the button on this page, or go to wa.me/15672352765, and say hi. No signup needed.

Is my data private?

Yes - see our Privacy Policy for exactly what Bud collects, how it's used, and how to delete it at any time.
